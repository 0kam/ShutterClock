## Errata v1.0 (fixed in v1.1)

1. **C1 silkscreen "+" is on the wrong pad.** `generate_board.py` assigns C1 pad 1 (the square
   pad, which the KiCad `CP_Radial` footprint marks "+") to GND and pad 2 to VIN. Electrically the
   board is fine; **mount C1 with its + lead in the right-hand (round) pad**. v1.1: swap the nets
   to `{"1": "VIN", "2": "GND"}` and re-route the two C1 tracks. C3 is correct (pad 1 = CAM_P).
2. **U2 (MBC2596-01) hole pattern is 40.0 x 17.5 mm** (measured on the real module 2026-10-19),
   not the 38 x 16 mm placeholder (±19.0 / ±8.0). v1.0 boards: the module is connected with bent
   tinned wire. v1.1: `fp_buck()` pads at ±20.0 / ±8.75 mm.
3. **F1 fuse clips (FUC-03A): the two pins of one clip lie ALONG the fuse axis**, not across it as
   in `fp_fuseclips()` (pairs 6.7 mm apart across the axis). v1.0 boards: cut one pin per clip and
   solder the other into one hole (keep the outer pin on one clip and the inner pin on the other
   so the clip spacing stays 18 mm), or put an inline fuse holder in the battery + lead and bridge
   the F1 holes. v1.1: rotate the footprint; pin pitch along the axis still to be measured.

## Firmware pin map (single source of truth for the board)

| XIAO pin | Net | Function |
|---|---|---|
| A0 (D0) | ADC_NODE | Battery voltage: VIN × 100k/(1M+100k) (ungated divider, 100 nF). Reading × 11 |
| A1 (D1) | A1_NODE | Camera current: 0.1 Ω low-side shunt in the camera return (CAM_N), 10 kΩ + 100 nF. 1 A = 0.1 V |
| D2 | AF_DRV | PC817 U4 (AF / half-press) via 1 kΩ. High = pressed |
| D7 | CAM_EN | Camera supply: Q3 → Q2 → MBC2596-01 (9.4 V) → 1N5822 → CAM_P. High = on |
| D8 | SHUT_DRV | PC817 U3 (SHUTTER / full-press) via 1 kΩ. High = pressed |
| D9 | BTN | Test-shot / BLE button SW1 to GND (enable the internal pull-up) |
| D10 | KA_EN | Keep-alive path: Q6 → Q5 → 10 Ω → Q7 follower (≈ 7.3–8.5 V) → CAM_P. High = on |
| 3V3 | 3V3_SYS | From U1 (NJW4181U3-33B) through JP1 |
| GND | GND | |
| D3–D6, 5V | — | unused (reachable on the XIAO castellations) |

Rules: never drive CAM_EN and KA_EN so that the camera is released on the keep-alive
path; enter keep-alive only from CAM_EN = on; drop to CAM_EN = on if A1 shows > 50 mA
during keep-alive; low-battery cutoff 11.5 V.

## Connectors and jumpers

- J1 (5.08 mm screw terminal, bottom-left): battery + / − (12 V lead-acid / LiFePO4; fuse F1 5 A, Q1 reverse protection, 2x P4KE15A TVS)
- J2 (5.08 mm screw terminal, bottom-right): camera + / − → Nikon EP-5B cable. The − side is the sense shunt — keep it as the only camera ground.
- J3 (3.5 mm stereo jack, right edge): tip = SHUTTER, ring = AF, sleeve = common → off-the-shelf 3.5 mm → MC-DC2 / 10-pin release cables (verify the cable's pinout with a meter)
- JP1 (near the XIAO): shunt fitted normally; **remove before connecting USB** to the XIAO (same rule as SnowGauge)
- JP2 (below Q3): shunt fitted = camera supply always on (bypasses Q3)
- U2: 4-pin module socket positions; set the trimpot to **9.4 V** and remove the module's LED before fitting

## Silkscreen notes

"REMOVE JP1 BEFORE USB", "U2: SET 9.4V, REMOVE LED", "JP2=ALWAYS ON", battery and camera polarity, diode cathodes marked "K".

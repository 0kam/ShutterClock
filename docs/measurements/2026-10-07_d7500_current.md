## What this means

- **Peak current.** Short peaks reach 3.1 A per shot and 3.9 A at power-on; ~26 ms per shot is
  above 2 A. A 2 A supply limit makes the camera fail ("Err"). The camera-supply path must be
  able to source ≈ 4 A peaks. The Pololu D36V28F9 (2.6 A typ. continuous) is marginal →
  check a larger module, a bulk capacitor on the 9 V side, or test the module on the bench
  (spec U9, new item).
- **Always-on vs power-per-shot (U8).** Camera floor 0.60 mA at 9 V = 5.4 mW ≈ 130 mWh/day,
  i.e. about as much as 15 shots/day. If the camera stays powered, the regulator's own
  quiescent current matters more than the camera (D36V28F9: 1–3 mA no-load at 12 V = 12–36 mW).
  Power-per-shot can also cut the ~11 s awake tail if power is removed right after the card
  write — but needs the boot cost (switch-ON event above: ~0.35 A·s in the first 0.4 s) and the
  auto-start behaviour (M4) to be measured.
- **Cheap savings in the camera menu.** Image review off and the shortest standby timer should
  shorten the tail; re-measure after changing them.

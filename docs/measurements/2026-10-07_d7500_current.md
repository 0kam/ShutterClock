# D7500 supply current — 2026-10-07

Spec §5 M1 (standby / off current), M2 (shot profile), M4 (power-up behaviour).
Spec U3, U4, U8, U9, U28.

Setup: bench PSU **9.00 V** → **picowatt** (INA228, 15 mΩ, ADCRANGE=0, `--preset fast`,
3200 Sa/s, stored zero/gain calibration applied) → EP-5B → **Nikon D7500**.
No ShutterClock hardware; shots released by hand with a wired remote. Image review
(playback menu) was **off** in all runs. Sensor cleaning setting: cleans at both power-switch
ON and OFF (seen in run 2).
PSU current limit 4 A. **With the limit at 2 A the camera showed "Err" and did not fire**
(user observation before run 1).

Command: `picowatt-cli --seconds N --csv runX.csv --preset fast --adcrange 0 --cal`.
Events below = current > 20 mA, gaps < 1 s merged. A·s = charge at 9 V (1 A·s = 0.278 mAh).

## Power-up behaviour (M4)

- PSU output off → the 9 V node decays over ~2.4 s (PSU output capacitance).
- PSU output on with the power switch ON: 62 mA for 0.54 s (boot), then awake as after a
  switch-ON but **without** the 2 s sensor cleaning; standby after 10.5 s.
- With the remote held fully pressed from power-up the shutter fired at **+1.95 s**
  (`data/2026-10-07_d7500_run4_powerup_shot_3200Hz.csv`). Firmware can therefore either wait
  ~2–2.5 s after enabling the supply, or assert AF + SHUTTER from power-up until the shot is
  detected. Tune with ShutterClock on the bench.
- Unknown: when the card write is really finished (needed before cutting power after a shot);
  whether the camera still releases when its clock has been reset and the date prompt is shown.

## What this means

- **Peak current (U28).** 3–4 A peaks, 2 A limit → "Err". The 9 V path must source ≥ 4 A peaks
  with margin. Pololu D36V28F9 (2.6 A typ.) is not enough; D36V50F9 (9 V, 5 A, same family,
  no-load 2–4 mA, sleep 10–20 µA/V) fits the peak.
- **Energy per day (U8)**, D7500, 6 shots/day, at 9 V:
  - always powered (switch ON, standby): 0.42 mA × 24 h + 6 × 2.7 A·s ≈ 10.1 + 4.5 =
    **≈ 15 mAh/day ≈ 0.13 Wh/day**, plus the regulator's own draw (D36V50F9 2–4 mA at 12 V ≈
    0.6–1.2 Wh/day — larger than the camera).
  - power per shot, cut right after the card write (~2.5 s on): ≈ 0.85 A·s per shot →
    **≈ 1.4 mAh/day ≈ 0.013 Wh/day** (+ regulator sleep unless a load switch is added).
  - For scale: a 12 V 20 Ah battery holds 240 Wh; a 10 W panel gives tens of Wh on a clear day.
    Both options are small against a solar-charged 12 V battery; they matter only when the
    panel is covered for months.
- **Cheap savings in the camera menu.** Standby timer 4 s instead of 6 s: −13 %. Image review
  was already off. For power-per-shot, set sensor cleaning to off (it does not run on a hard
  power-up anyway, but runs on every switch ON/OFF).
